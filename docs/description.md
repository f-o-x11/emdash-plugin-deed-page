deed.page Publisher adds a **deed.page** panel to the EmDash content editor. One click turns the saved version of a post or page into a standalone, fast-loading page at `https://your-name.deed.page`.

- **Publish** an entry under a name you choose.
- **Update** it later with one click, or automatically whenever the entry is saved while published.
- **Delete** it from deed.page without touching the EmDash entry.
- See everything you have published on the plugin's **deed.page** admin page.

The page is plain HTML: title, summary, body (Portable Text paragraphs, headings, lists, quotes, links, images, code), and a link back to the original when the entry is public on your site (`rel="canonical"`).

No deed.page account is needed. The first publish creates a free deed.page owner token and saves it, encrypted, in the plugin settings.
