### Does it cost anything?

No. The plugin is free and MIT licensed, and the deed.page free plan covers up to 25 sites and 60 publishes per hour. If deed.page reports a plan limit, the plugin shows its message as is; nothing is bought automatically.

### Where is the deed.page token stored?

In the plugin's `token` setting, which EmDash encrypts at rest. It is never logged or shown in the UI. Keep your own copy (paste it into Settings to replace it): deed.page shows a new token only once, and it is the only way to manage the sites outside EmDash.

### Does it publish drafts?

It publishes the saved version of the entry when you click Publish or Update. The panel warns you when the entry is not yet published on your site. Automatic updates only run for entries that are published.

### What happens when I delete an entry in EmDash?

Nothing on deed.page. Use **Delete from deed.page** in the panel first if you want the page gone.

### Can I change the name later?

Delete the deed.page copy, then publish again under a new name.
